Your Workshop Journey - From Ideas to Results

Every organization starts in a different place. These workshops are designed to help you move from early exploration to a working prototype - without long strategy phases or complexity. Choose the format that matches where your team is right now.

Workshops
#0 Initial Alignment Call +

A free 30-minute call to understand your current situation, challenges, and goals. We'll identify the right starting point for your AI or Cloud journey.

#1 Discovery Workshop - Identify Use Cases +

A half-day workshop to map your processes, identify AI/Cloud opportunities, and prioritize use cases based on business value and feasibility.

#2 Prototype Workshop - Design & Prioritize +

A full-day hands-on workshop where we design and build a working prototype together. You'll leave with something tangible to show stakeholders.

#3 Validation Workshop - Build & Validate +

A multi-day engagement to validate your prototype with real users, gather feedback, and create a roadmap for production implementation.
