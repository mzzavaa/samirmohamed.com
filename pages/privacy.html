Data Privacy Policy

Overview

This website collects minimal data. We don't use cookies for tracking, we don't collect personal information, and we don't share any data with third parties.

Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect any personal data. It only tracks aggregated, anonymous usage statistics such as page views and referrer information.

Plausible is fully compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR. No consent banner is required because no personal data is collected.

For more information, see the Plausible Data Policy.

Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to the newsletter, your email address will be stored and processed for the purpose of sending you updates. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in every email.

External Links

This website contains links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.
