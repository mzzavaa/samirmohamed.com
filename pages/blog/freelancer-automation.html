Automating the Freelance Grind

Jul 20, 2026 · 3 min read

🔗 Check it out: freelancer-automation.com

Freelancing is great until you realize half your time goes to admin work. Tracking hours, sending invoices, chasing payments, updating spreadsheets.

I automated most of it. Here's how.

The Problem

Every month was the same:

  • Export time entries from 3 different tools
  • Manually calculate totals per client
  • Create invoices in Word
  • Send emails
  • Track who paid, who didn't
  • Send reminders

Took 4-5 hours per month. Every month. For years.

The Solution

Scripts. Lots of scripts.

Time tracking syncs to a central place. End of month, one command generates all invoices. Another command emails them. Payment reminders go out automatically after 14 days.

What's On the Site

Guides and tools for freelancers who want to automate their admin:

  • Time tracking setups that don't suck
  • Invoice generation scripts
  • Payment tracking automations
  • Client onboarding workflows
  • Tax prep helpers (EU focused)

Time Saved

Before: 4-5 hours/month on admin.

After: Maybe 30 minutes reviewing what the scripts did.

That's 50+ hours per year. A full work week, back in my pocket.

The Catch

Setup takes time. You need to invest a few hours upfront to save hours later. Most people won't do it.

If you're billing decent rates, automating admin is one of the best ROI investments you can make.
